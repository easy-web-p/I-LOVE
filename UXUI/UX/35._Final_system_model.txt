User
├── owns Memories
├── owns Important Dates
├── owns Media
├── owns Projects
└── receives Entitlements

Project
├── selects one Template
├── contains ordered Sections
├── references Memories
├── references Media
├── has Privacy Settings
└── creates a Published Snapshot

Published Snapshot
├── is accessed through a Slug
├── contains sanitized public content
├── enforces access rules
├── may have reveal and expiration times
└── produces analytics
